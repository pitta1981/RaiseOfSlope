 QGIS plugin for DEM profile extraction, slope stability analysis (grid/simplex) with limit equilibrium method, stratigraphy and groundwater handling, and results export as image, report and DXF. 
 
 If you use this plugin, please cite these two papers:
 [1] Pittaluga, S., Lalicata, L.M., Bressan, A., Tamellini, L., Gallipoli, D., Large-Scale Slope Equilibrium Analysis Within a GIS Framework. In: Monaco, P., Chiaradonna, A. (eds) Prediction and Performance in Geotechnical Engineering -  Proceedings of the 9th Italian National Conference of the Researchers of Geotechnical Engineering CNRIG 2026. Springer Series in Geomechanics and Geoengineering. Springer, Cham. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-30669-2_14
 [2] Lalicata, L.M., Bressan, A., Pittaluga, S., Tamellini, L., Gallipoli, D., An Efficient Slope Stability Algorithm with Physically Consistent Parametrisation of Slip Surfaces. Int J Civ Eng 23, 671-682 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1007/s40999-024-01053-1
